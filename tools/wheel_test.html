Calibrate the wheel

1. Measure one full turn (do this first): line A up under the window with the nudge buttons, click Start, then nudge the wheel one full turn (start with Full turn, then ++ / +) until A is back under the window, and click End.
2. Fine-tune letters: click a letter above, nudge it until it sits exactly under the window, click Fix this letter.
